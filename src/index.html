A non-functioning company website can lead to significant losses
You may not even know that your website is down (even for a short time)
Loss of Clients and Revenue
In a highly competitive environment, an inaccessible website can lead to a significant loss of clients. Buyers expect instant access to information and products. If your website is unavailable, they will quickly move to competitors, resulting in direct losses
Damage to Reputation
Business reputation plays a key role. A non-functioning website can cause negative reviews and comments on social networks and online platforms. One bad impression can drive away many potential clients
SEO and Ranking Issues
Search engine algorithms take website availability into account when ranking. Frequent downtime can lead to lower positions in search results, reducing organic traffic and making it harder to attract new clients
Data Security
Under strict data protection standards, a non-functioning or vulnerable website can become an easy target for hackers. A breach of confidential information and client data not only violates data protection laws but also seriously undermines client trust
Loss of Competitive Advantage
Today's market demands constant readiness from businesses. Every minute of your website's downtime is an opportunity for competitors to win over your clients. Quick detection and resolution of issues allow you to maintain a leading position
Financial and Legal Risks
Website downtime can lead to significant financial losses not only due to missed profits but also due to potential lawsuits and fines. Clients and partners have high expectations for the reliability and availability of online services
Loss of Trust from Investors and Partners
Investors and partners value stability and reliability. Constant issues with website availability can erode their trust, negatively impacting funding and growth opportunities for your business
Monitor Key Site Metrics
Our monitoring system helps you keep track of your websites' status. After registration, you can add sites for
regular monitoring.
  • General Availability
    Ensures that your website is accessible and all its features are working correctly
  • System Errors
    Detects and alerts you about any system errors that could impact the user experience or site performance
  • Google Safe Browsing alerts
    Verifies that your website is not listed as unsafe by Google Safe Browsing, protecting your visitors from potential threats
  • SSL Certificate Validity
    Checks the status and expiration date of your SSL certificate to ensure secure communication and avoid disruptions
  • Domain Expiration Date
    Monitors the expiration date of your domain to prevent unintentional downtime due to expired domain registration
  • Check up every 15 minutes
    Conducts regular checks every 15 minutes to promptly detect and respond to any issues that may arise
  • Availability Checks in Different Countries
    Verifies your website’s availability from multiple locations worldwide to ensure global accessibility and performance
  • PageSpeed Insights
    Analyze your website's loading speed and identify key metrics to improve user experience and SEO performance
  • Redirect Checker
    Verify the accuracy and status codes of your website's redirects to ensure seamless navigation and avoid broken links
